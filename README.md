# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transactional data from 3,900 purchases across multiple product categories. The objective is to uncover customer spending patterns, purchasing preferences, subscription trends, and business opportunities through data analysis and visualization.

The project combines **Python**, **PostgreSQL**, and **Power BI** to perform end-to-end data analytics, from data cleaning and transformation to business intelligence reporting.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing (Python)

---

## Key Insights

### Revenue by Gender

Male customers generated significantly higher revenue than female customers.

### Customer Segmentation

Most customers belong to the Loyal segment, indicating strong repeat purchasing behavior.

### Top-Rated Products

Products such as Gloves, Sandals, and Boots received the highest average ratings.

### Subscription Analysis

Non-subscribers generated higher overall revenue due to their larger customer base.

### Age Group Performance

Young Adult customers contributed the highest revenue among all age groups.

### Shipping Preferences

Express shipping customers showed slightly higher average purchase amounts.
